When Bloom Cosmetics approached us, their website was generating plenty of traffic but failing to convert. Despite 50K monthly visitors, their conversion rate hovered around 1.2% — well below the industry average for beauty brands.

### The Challenge

The site was built on an outdated Magento platform, resulting in:
- **Slow page loads:** Average load time of 6.8 seconds
- **Poor mobile experience:** 72% of traffic was mobile, but the site wasn't optimized
- **Confusing navigation:** Users struggled to find products
- **Cluttered checkout:** 64% cart abandonment rate

### Our Approach

We took a data-first approach to the redesign:

1. **User Research** — We analyzed heatmaps, session recordings, and customer surveys to identify friction points
2. **Information Architecture** — Simplified the product hierarchy from 12 categories to 6 clear collections
3. **Performance First** — Migrated to Next.js with Shopify headless commerce for sub-second load times
4. **Mobile-First Design** — Built for the 72% of users browsing on phones
5. **Streamlined Checkout** — Reduced checkout from 5 steps to 2, with guest checkout option

### The Results

After launching the redesigned site:

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Conversion Rate | 1.2% | 1.68% | **+40%** |
| Page Load Time | 6.8s | 1.2s | **-82%** |
| Cart Abandonment | 64% | 41% | **-36%** |
| Mobile Revenue | $12K/mo | $28K/mo | **+133%** |

### Key Takeaways

This project reinforced that **performance is a feature, not an afterthought**. Every 100ms improvement in load time contributed to measurable conversion gains. Additionally, understanding your users' journey — not just their demographics — is the difference between a pretty website and one that drives revenue.
