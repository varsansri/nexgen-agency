Mobile apps have evolved from novelty to necessity. With the average person spending 4.5 hours on their phone daily and mobile commerce projected to reach $710 billion by the end of 2026, businesses without a mobile strategy are leaving money on the table.

### The Mobile Opportunity

Consider these statistics:
- **79%** of smartphone users have made a purchase using their mobile device in the last 6 months
- Mobile apps convert **3x higher** than mobile websites
- Push notifications increase retention by **3-10x** compared to email
- Users spend **7x more time** in apps than on mobile websites

### When Does Your Business Need an App?

Not every business needs a mobile app. You should consider one if:

1. **Repeat engagement** — Customers interact with you more than once a week
2. **Personalized experiences** — You can deliver value through user-specific content
3. **Offline capability** — Your users need access without an internet connection
4. **Device features** — You can leverage camera, GPS, or push notifications
5. **Customer loyalty** — You want to build a direct, persistent relationship
