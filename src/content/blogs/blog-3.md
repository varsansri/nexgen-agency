Search is evolving faster than ever. With Google's AI Overviews now mainstream and user expectations shifting, the old SEO playbook needs updating. Here's what's working now and what to prepare for.

### 1. AI Overviews Are Here to Stay

Google's AI-generated search summaries now appear for the majority of informational queries. This changes the game:

- **Zero-click searches are increasing** — Optimize for featured snippets and AI overview inclusion
- **Long-tail, conversational queries matter more** — Structure content around natural language questions
- **E-E-A-T signals are critical** — Demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authority, and Trustworthiness

### 2. Topical Authority Over Keyword Density

Google now evaluates whether your site has genuine expertise on a topic, not just whether you used the right keywords.

**What to do:**
- Create comprehensive topic clusters, not isolated blog posts
- Cover related subtopics thoroughly
- Interlink content strategically within clusters

### 3. Core Web Vitals Still Rule

Page experience remains a direct ranking factor. In 2026, the bar is higher:

- **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):** Under 2.5 seconds
- **INP (Interaction to Next Paint):** Under 200ms
- **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift):** Under 0.1

### 4. Video SEO Is Non-Negotiable

With YouTube being the second-largest search engine and short-form video dominating social, optimizing video content is essential:

- Create video versions of your top blog posts
- Use YouTube chapters, transcripts, and keyword-rich descriptions
- Embed videos on your site to increase dwell time

### 5. Local SEO for Service Businesses

For agencies and service businesses targeting specific markets:
- Optimize your Google Business Profile
- Collect and respond to reviews actively
- Build location-specific landing pages

### The Bottom Line

SEO in 2026 is about being the best answer to a user's question, delivered fast, with demonstrated expertise. Technical optimization, content quality, and authority signals work together — none alone is sufficient.
